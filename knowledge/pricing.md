# Pricing and Packages

All prices are in US dollars. Every package has a one-time setup fee and a monthly fee. The final quote depends on how much content you have and which integrations you need.

## Starter package
A website chatbot that answers from your content.
- Price: from $499 setup + $99 per month
- Website chat widget
- Up to 50 documents
- Answers with sources
- Human handoff to your team
- Launch in about 7 days

## Growth package
Website and WhatsApp assistant connected to your CRM. Our most popular package.
- Price: from $1,499 setup + $299 per month
- Everything in the Starter package
- WhatsApp assistant
- CRM integration
- Lead capture from chats
- Multilingual replies
- WhatsApp messaging fees charged by Meta are billed at cost

## Custom package
Agentic RAG across your whole business.
- Price: from $4,999, quoted per project
- Agentic RAG workflows
- Multiple sources and channels
- Custom integrations
- Internal team assistant
- Dedicated setup support

## What the monthly fee covers
Hosting, AI usage within fair limits, content updates, monitoring and support.
