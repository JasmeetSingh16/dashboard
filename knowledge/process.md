# How Jaseir Sets Up Your AI Assistant

## Step 1: Connect your content

You share the content the assistant should answer from. This can be documents (PDF, Word), website pages, FAQs, product catalogs, price lists, policies, SOPs or a Google Drive folder. You decide exactly what is included; the assistant never answers from anything outside the approved content.

## Step 2: Understand and index

Jaseir cleans the content, splits it into short passages and indexes them so they can be searched both by meaning and by exact keywords. Each passage keeps a reference to its document and section, so answers can cite their source.

## Step 3: Retrieve

When someone asks a question, the assistant searches the index and picks the few passages most likely to contain the answer. Follow-up questions are understood in the context of the conversation.

## Step 4: Answer or hand off

The assistant writes a short answer in plain language using only the retrieved passages and shows the source. If the passages don't contain the answer, it says so and offers to connect the person with your team, for example through a booking link, WhatsApp or email.

## Testing before launch

Before going live, Jaseir tests the assistant with your most common customer questions and with questions it should refuse. You review the answers, and we adjust the content or settings until you are happy.

## Launch and channels

The assistant can be launched as a website chat widget first, then extended to WhatsApp, Instagram direct messages and an internal staff assistant. The same knowledge powers every channel.

## Keeping the knowledge up to date

When your prices, policies or products change, you update the source documents and the assistant is re-indexed. Unchanged documents are skipped, so updates are quick.

## Setup time

The Starter package usually launches in about 7 days after we receive your content and common questions. Growth and Custom setups take longer because they include more channels and integrations; we agree a timeline before starting.
