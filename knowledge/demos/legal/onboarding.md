# Onboarding FAQ

## How do I become a client?

Book an introductory call, sign the engagement letter and upload your documents through the secure client portal.

## How long does onboarding take?

About one week after the firm receives your documents.

## Is my information confidential?

Yes. Client information is kept confidential and documents are shared only through the secure client portal.

## Can the assistant give legal or tax advice?

No. The assistant answers from the firm's general information only. For advice on your own situation, speak with a professional at the firm.
