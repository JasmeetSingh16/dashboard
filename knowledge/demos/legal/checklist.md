# Document Checklist for Annual Tax Filing

Sample content for a demo. This is not legal or tax advice.

## Documents we need for your annual tax filing

From the firm's checklist, your annual tax filing needs: photo ID, income statements, bank statements and receipts for deductions.

## Income statements

Salary slips and annual income statements from employers, plus statements for any freelance or rental income.

## Receipts for deductions

Receipts for donations and for eligible education, medical or home-office expenses, depending on your situation.

## How to send your documents

Upload them through the firm's secure client portal. Scans or clear photos are accepted.
