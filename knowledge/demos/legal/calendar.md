# Filing Calendar & Reminders

Sample dates for a demo. This is not legal or tax advice.

## Key dates (sample)

- Send us your documents by 31 January.
- We share your draft return by 15 March.
- Filing deadline: 15 April.
- Quarterly estimated payments: 15 April, 15 June, 15 September and 15 January.

## Reminders

The firm sends email reminders 30 days and 7 days before each date.

## If you miss a deadline

Contact the firm as soon as possible. Late filing can lead to penalties, and the team will explain your options.
