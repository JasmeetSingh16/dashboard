# Salon FAQ

## Can I choose my stylist?

Yes. Choose your preferred stylist when you book, or pick "first available".

## Do you offer packages for weddings and events?

Yes. Hair, makeup and nail packages for weddings and events are available on request.

## Which products do you use?

Professional salon products. Ask at reception if you need ingredient information.

## Do you sell gift vouchers?

Yes, at reception or online.
