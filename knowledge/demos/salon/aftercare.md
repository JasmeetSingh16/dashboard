# Patch Test & Aftercare

## Patch test before hair colour

Yes, you need a quick patch test 48 hours before your colour appointment. It takes 5 minutes and you can book it online.

## Why a patch test is needed

It checks for an allergic reaction to the colour products before your appointment.

## Hair colour aftercare

Wait 48 hours before washing your hair and use a colour-safe shampoo to keep the colour longer.

## After a facial

Avoid makeup for 12 hours and use sunscreen for the next few days.

## Product advice

Stylists recommend products matched to your hair or skin type. They are available at reception.
