# Booking, Timings & Cancellations

## Opening hours

Tuesday to Saturday 9:00–19:00 and Sunday 10:00–16:00. Closed on Mondays.

## Booking an appointment

Book online or through this assistant. Walk-ins are welcome when a stylist is free.

## Cancellation policy

Please give at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule. Late cancellations and no-shows may be charged 50% of the service price.

## Running late

If you are more than 15 minutes late, your appointment may need to be rescheduled.
