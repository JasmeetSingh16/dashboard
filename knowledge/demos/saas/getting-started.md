# Getting Started & Integrations

Our app is a project-management tool for teams. This is sample demo content.

## Creating your workspace

Sign up with your work email, name your workspace and invite teammates from Settings → Members. A workspace can hold as many projects as your plan allows.

## Creating your first project

Click New project and choose a template: board, list or timeline. Add tasks with assignees, due dates, labels and subtasks.

## Connecting Google Calendar

To connect Google Calendar, go to Settings → Integrations → Google Calendar and click Connect. Sign in with your Google account and allow access. Syncing takes about a minute. After that, tasks with due dates appear in your calendar automatically.

## Other integrations

The app can also turn forwarded emails into tasks and send events to webhooks for custom automations. All integrations are managed in Settings → Integrations.

## Mobile apps

The app has mobile apps for iOS and Android. Sign in with the same account to see all your projects.
