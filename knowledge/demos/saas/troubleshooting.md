# Troubleshooting

## Google Calendar is not syncing

Only tasks with a due date sync to your calendar, and a new connection takes about a minute. If tasks still don't appear, go to Settings → Integrations → Google Calendar, click Disconnect, then click Connect again.

## The app is not loading or not syncing

Refresh the page and check your internet connection. If the problem continues, sign out and sign back in. On mobile, update the app to the latest version.

## I can't log in

Use the "Forgot password" link on the sign-in page. The reset email arrives within 5 minutes; check your spam folder if you don't see it.

## I'm not getting notifications

Check Settings → Notifications and make sure email or push notifications are turned on for the projects you follow.

## Tasks are missing from a project

Check that no filters are active and that the project isn't archived. Archived projects are listed under Projects → Archived.
