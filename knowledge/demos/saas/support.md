# Support & Handoff

## Contacting support

Open the Help menu in the app and choose Contact support. Live chat support is available Monday to Friday, 9:00–18:00 UTC.

## Response times

- Free plan: within 2 business days.
- Team plan: within 1 business day.
- Business plan: priority support, within 4 hours on business days.

## When the assistant hands you to a person

If the assistant can't find the answer in the help center, it passes the conversation to a support agent together with the chat history, so you don't have to repeat yourself.

## Reporting a bug

Go to Help → Report a bug and describe what happened. Screenshots help the team fix issues faster.
