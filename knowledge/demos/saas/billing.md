# Plans & Billing

## Plans

- Free: up to 3 projects and 5 members.
- Team: $8 per user per month. Unlimited projects, timeline view and integrations.
- Business: $15 per user per month. Everything in Team plus admin controls, single sign-on and priority support.

## Free trial

Every new workspace gets a 14-day free trial of the Team plan. No credit card is needed.

## Billing and invoices

You can pay monthly or yearly; yearly billing saves 20%. Invoices are available in Settings → Billing. Payment is by credit or debit card.

## Changing or cancelling your plan

You can upgrade or downgrade at any time and the change is prorated. To cancel, go to Settings → Billing → Cancel plan. You keep access until the end of the current billing period.

## Refunds

Yearly plans can be refunded within 30 days of purchase. Monthly plans are not refunded, but you can cancel before the next billing date.
