# Shipping & Order Tracking

## Shipping times

Standard shipping takes 3–5 business days. Express shipping takes 1–2 business days.

## Shipping costs

Standard shipping is free on orders over $50; otherwise it costs $4.95. Express shipping costs $12.95.

## Tracking your order

When your order ships, you receive an email with a tracking link. You can also track it under My account → Orders.

## Where we ship

We ship to addresses in the United States and Canada.

## Changing or cancelling an order

You can change or cancel an order within 1 hour of placing it under My account → Orders. After that, the order is already being packed.
