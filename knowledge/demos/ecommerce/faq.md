# Store FAQ

## Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept major credit and debit cards and our store gift cards.

## Do you sell gift cards?

Yes. Digital gift cards are available from $25 to $200 and never expire.

## Do you have discounts?

Sign up for the newsletter to get 10% off your first order.

## Do I need an account to order?

No. You can check out as a guest, but an account makes tracking and returns easier.

## When is customer service available?

Chat support is available Monday to Saturday, 8:00–20:00.
