# Products & Sizes

Our store is an online clothing and home store. This is sample demo content.

## What we sell

Clothing for women and men, shoes, and home textiles such as bedding and towels.

## Size guide

Clothing comes in sizes XS to XXL. Measure your chest, waist and hips and compare them with the size chart on each product page. If you are between two sizes, choose the larger size for a relaxed fit.

## Fit notes

Jeans run slightly small, so many customers order one size up. T-shirts and sweaters are true to size.

## Stock

Product pages show stock for each size. For sold-out sizes you can click "Notify me" to get an email when the size is back.

## Materials and care

Care instructions are on each product page and on the label. Most cotton items can be machine-washed at 30 °C.
