# Site Visits

## Booking a site visit

Site visits are available Monday to Saturday, 10:00–17:00. Book through this assistant or on our website. A visit takes about 45 minutes with a sales advisor.

## Show apartment

You can tour a fully furnished 2-bedroom show apartment during your visit.

## Safety on site

Please wear closed shoes. Hard hats are provided for the construction areas.

## Virtual tour

Can't visit in person? A virtual tour of the show apartment is available on our website.
