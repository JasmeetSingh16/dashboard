# Prices & Floor Plans

## Prices for this project

- 1-bedroom apartments: from $210,000
- 2-bedroom apartments: from $320,000
- 3-bedroom apartments: from $450,000

Apartments with 2 or more bedrooms include one underground parking space.

## Floor plans

1-bedroom apartments are about 55 m², 2-bedroom apartments about 82 m² and 3-bedroom apartments about 115 m². Detailed floor plans are sent by email after you register your interest.

## Payment plan

10% on booking, 20% when the purchase contract is signed and the remaining 70% at handover.

## What's included

Kitchen appliances, built-in wardrobes and air conditioning in all rooms.
