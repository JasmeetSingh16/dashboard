# Buyer FAQ

## Can international buyers purchase an apartment?

Yes. International buyers are welcome, and our sales team explains each step of the process.

## Can you help with a mortgage?

We can introduce you to lenders who offer mortgages for new-build apartments.

## Is there a warranty?

Yes. Every apartment comes with a 10-year structural warranty.

## When will I get the keys?

Handover is planned for Q4, as listed in the project brochure. Buyers are informed of their exact handover date three months in advance.
