# Patient FAQ

## Do I need a referral?

No. You don't need a referral for general or dental appointments.

## How do I get my test results?

Results are posted on the patient portal within 24 hours. The doctor contacts you directly if anything needs follow-up.

## Do you treat children?

Yes. Our doctors see children of all ages, and dental care is available from age 3.

## Is parking available?

Yes, free parking is available for patients.

## Is this an emergency clinic?

No. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
