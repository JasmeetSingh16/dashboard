# Timings & Appointments

## Opening hours

Monday to Friday 8:00–19:00 and Saturday 9:00–14:00. Closed on Sundays.

## Booking an appointment

Book online or through this assistant. Same-day appointments are often available in the afternoon.

## Morning slots for fasting tests

Fasting blood tests are done in morning slots between 7:00 and 10:00 on weekdays.

## Cancelling or rescheduling

Please cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours before your appointment. Late cancellations are charged a $20 fee.

## Before you arrive

Arrive 10 minutes early and bring a photo ID and your insurance card.
