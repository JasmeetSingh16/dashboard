# Services & Tests

The clinic is a family and dental clinic. This is sample demo content.

## General consultations

Our doctors see adults and children for general health questions, check-ups and follow-ups.

## Dental care

Check-ups, cleaning, fillings and teeth whitening.

## Blood tests

We offer blood sugar (glucose) tests, cholesterol tests and full blood counts. Results are available on the patient portal within 24 hours.

## Preparing for a blood sugar test

Yes — you need to fast for 8 to 10 hours before a blood sugar test, and water is fine. Avoid food, coffee, tea and juice. Fasting tests are done in morning slots between 7:00 and 10:00, and the assistant can help you book a morning slot.

## Preparing for a cholesterol test

Fasting for 9 to 12 hours is recommended before a cholesterol test. Water is fine.

## Vaccinations

Seasonal flu vaccinations are available from October to March.
