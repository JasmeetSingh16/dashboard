# Insurance & Payments

## Insurance

We accept most major insurance plans. Send us your plan details before your visit and we will confirm your coverage.

## Self-pay prices

- General consultation: $60
- Dental check-up and cleaning: $90
- Blood sugar test: $25

## Payment methods

We accept credit and debit cards and cash. Payment is taken at the end of your visit.

## Receipts

You receive an itemized receipt that you can use for insurance claims.
