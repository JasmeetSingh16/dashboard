# Data Privacy and Security

## How your content is used

Your documents and knowledge are used only to answer questions for your business. They are never used to train public AI models.

## Answers only from approved content

The assistant answers only from the content you approve. Text inside your documents is treated as information, not as instructions, so a document cannot change how the assistant behaves.

## Data hosting

Data can be hosted in the EU or the US, depending on your requirements.

## GDPR

The setup is designed to be GDPR-ready: you control what content is included, conversations can be reviewed, and personal data is kept to what is needed.

## Deleting your data

You can ask for your content and conversation data to be deleted at any time.

## Conversation logs

Conversations can be logged so you can review answer quality and see which sources were used. Logs are kept for your business only.

## Human handoff

When the assistant is not confident an answer is in your content, it hands the conversation to your team instead of guessing.
