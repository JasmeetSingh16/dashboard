# Frequently Asked Questions

## What is RAG, in simple words?

RAG stands for retrieval-augmented generation. Before the AI answers, it first looks up the most relevant parts of your own documents, and then writes the answer using only that information. It is like giving the AI an open-book exam where the book is your business's content.

## How does it work?

It works in three steps, which is what RAG means. Retrieve: it finds the most relevant passages in your documents. Augment: it gives those passages to the AI as its only source of truth. Generate: it writes a clear answer and shows exactly where it came from. If the answer isn't in your content, it hands the conversation to your team.

## What sources can the assistant use?

The assistant can answer from documents (PDF, Word, text files), website pages, FAQs, product catalogs, price lists, policies, SOPs and Google Drive folders. You decide exactly which content is included, and it never answers from anything outside that content.

## How is this different from ChatGPT?

A general chatbot like ChatGPT answers from general internet knowledge and can guess when it isn't sure. Jaseir's knowledge assistant answers only from your approved content, shows the source for every answer, and hands the conversation to your team when the answer isn't in your content.

## What happens if the AI doesn't know the answer?

It says honestly that it doesn't have that information and offers to connect the person with your team, for example through a booking link, WhatsApp or email. It does not make up an answer.

## Does it work on WhatsApp and Instagram?

Yes. The same assistant can run on your website, on WhatsApp and in Instagram direct messages, and as an internal assistant for your staff.

## Does it work in other languages?

Yes. The assistant replies in the language the customer writes in, even if your documents are in a different language.

## Can it capture leads?

Yes. The assistant can collect contact details during a conversation, and with the Lead Qualification agent it can score the lead, update your CRM and alert your sales team.

## Can I control what the assistant says?

Yes. It only answers from the content you approve. You can add, remove or update documents at any time, and you can review conversations to see which sources were used.

## Can my staff use it too?

Yes. An internal team assistant lets staff find answers in SOPs, policies and process documents, with the page each answer came from.
