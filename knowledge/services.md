# Jaseir Services

Jaseir builds AI and growth systems for businesses. We focus on practical tools that connect to the software a team already uses, rather than replacing it.

## AI knowledge assistant (RAG chatbot)

The AI knowledge assistant answers questions from customers and staff using the business's own content: documents, website pages, FAQs, SOPs, product catalogs and policies. It uses retrieval-augmented generation (RAG): before answering, it searches the approved content for the most relevant passages and writes the answer only from those passages.

Every answer shows the source it came from, such as the document name and section. If the answer is not in the approved content, the assistant says it doesn't know and offers to connect the person with the team instead of guessing.

The assistant can run as a chat widget on a website, on WhatsApp, in Instagram direct messages, and as an internal assistant for staff.

## AI agents and automation

Jaseir builds AI agents that take action after a conversation, not just answer questions. Examples include:

- **Lead qualification agent:** reads a conversation or form, scores how likely the lead is to buy, updates the CRM and alerts the sales team for high-intent leads.
- **SEO and content planning agents:** audit a website, find visibility gaps and turn them into a prioritized plan.
- **Conversion friction analysis:** reviews pages and customer journeys to find what stops visitors from converting.
- **Competitor comparison:** compares positioning, content and offers across competitor websites.

Agents can be combined with the knowledge assistant, so a customer gets an answer and the team gets a qualified lead automatically.

## CRM and workflow automation

Jaseir connects CRMs, forms, email, messaging tools and spreadsheets into reliable workflows. Typical workflows include routing new leads, sending follow-ups, enriching contact records and keeping teams in sync.

## SEO and AI search visibility

Jaseir improves how a business appears in search engines and in AI answer engines. Work includes technical SEO, content strategy, structured data and visibility analysis.

## Web development and ecommerce

Jaseir designs and builds fast, conversion-focused websites and ecommerce stores, with the integrations the business needs.

## Industries we work with

The knowledge assistant is most useful for teams that answer the same questions every day. Common examples are SaaS and customer support teams, ecommerce stores, clinics, real estate businesses, law and accounting firms, and agencies. It can be set up for any business that has its answers written down somewhere.

## Languages

The assistant is multilingual. Customers can ask in the language they are most comfortable with, and the assistant replies in that same language. The source documents can be in one language while the conversation happens in another.

## Integrations

The assistant connects to the tools your business already uses:
- CRMs: HubSpot, Zoho CRM, Salesforce, Pipedrive
- Spreadsheets and databases: Google Sheets, Airtable
- Automation: Zapier, Make, n8n
- Messaging: WhatsApp Business, Slack, email
- Websites: WordPress, Webflow, Shopify, or any site via a one-line script

Need another tool? If it has an API, we can usually connect it.
